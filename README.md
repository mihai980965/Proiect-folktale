# Project-folktale
WordQuest is a terminal-based word puzzle game built in Python. The goal is to unscramble letters to form words, earn coins for every correct answer, and progress through increasingly difficult levels.

Each level challenges your vocabulary and ability to recognize words from scrambled letters. Collect enough coins to unlock the final level, where a new challenge awaits.

The goal is simple: solve the words, collect coins, unlock the final challenge, and complete the game.
